## Who we're talking to
1. **Resellers:** merchant branch managers, showroom owners, tile and waterproofing suppliers. They care about margin, reliable supply, a tight range that covers the job, and support for their counter staff.
2. **Specifiers:** architects, interior designers, hydraulic consultants. They care about NCC 2022 and livable housing (level access), the QBuild Social Housing Specification, spec sheets, CAD details, finish consistency and custom options.
3. **Builders and developers:** zero-threshold entries, balconies, multi-residential, social housing. They care about compliance, buildability, lead times and cost.
4. **Plumbers, tilers and waterproofers:** they care about falls, outlet sizing, cutting on site, sealing (e.g. Sikaflex 11FC), and tile-insert depth (up to 10mm tile).
5. **Homeowners and renovators:** an influence audience in Phase 1 (pointed to resellers), and a buying audience from Phase 2.

## Brand voice
Technical, precise, calm, architectural. Like an experienced hydraulic or plumbing specialist who also cares how it looks.
- **Do:** use real numbers (mm, DN sizes, grades, falls, tile thicknesses); name the standard or spec you mean; explain trade-offs honestly (304 vs 316, linear vs square); use Australian English (colour, metre, aluminium, fall, waste); keep sentences short.
- **Don't:** use hype ("game-changer", "revolutionary", "best in Australia"), fake urgency, exclamation marks, emoji (social at most one), or US spellings. Don't oversell compliance: products help a design comply, but the installation and the whole system have to comply too.
- **Visual identity (from the site):** dark "ink/night" and "paper/stone" palette with brass accents (#B08D57); headings in Montserrat 600, body text in Geist, small technical labels in Geist Mono. Finish swatches: brass #B08D57, bronze #7B5A43, gun metal #4A4B4D, black #1E1E1E, stainless #C9CACC.

## Compliance rules (non-negotiable)
- **Australian Consumer Law:** no misleading claims. No "best", "cheapest" or "lowest price" without a written, checkable basis. No invented reference prices or "was/now" pricing.
- **Prices:** quote only RRP inc GST from the current Neston price list in Google Drive, and check it against the live product. Never publish trade or reseller prices.
- **Technical and regulatory claims:** NCC 2022, AS/NZS 3500 (plumbing and drainage), AS 3740 (waterproofing of wet areas), AS 4654 (external waterproofing), livable housing design, the QBuild Social Housing Specification, slip resistance (AS 4586), load class. Cite one only where it genuinely applies. Never say a product "is NCC compliant" on its own: say what requirement it helps meet. Quote a slip rating, load class or flow rate only if a technical document states it.
- **WaterMark:** only as stated in the product data or technical documents.
- **Installation:** drainage work in Australia must be done by a licensed plumber; waterproofing is done to AS 3740 by a qualified applicator. Say so where it's relevant.
- **Warranty:** TBC. Don't mention one.
- **Testimonials and case studies:** only real, permission-given ones.
- **Email/SMS:** Spam Act 2003, so consent is required, the sender must be identified, and there must be a working unsubscribe. Applies to B2B email too.
- **Channel rule:** in Phase 1, no consumer-facing discounts or direct-buy pushes.
