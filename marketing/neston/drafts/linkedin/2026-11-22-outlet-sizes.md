## Main post (Angle: belief break)

DN40 isn't 40mm.

On a shower drain, the DN number is a nominal size label, not a measurement. On Neston drains the outlets are:

DN100 → 89mm
DN80 → 76mm
DN40 → 38mm

The one that catches people out is DN40. It isn't meant to go into 40mm pipe. It suits 50mm pipe with a 50mm puddle flange that has a 40mm internal throat. Get that wrong and you find out on site.
