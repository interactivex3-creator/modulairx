## 2. Business description (≤750 chars)
> Neston Architectural Drains designs and supplies premium 316 stainless steel drainage for bathrooms and outdoor spaces. The range covers linear shower drains with tile insert and wedge wire grates (800–2000mm), 120mm square tile insert wastes, and heel-proof wedge wire drains for balconies, pool surrounds and alfresco areas, in five matched PVD finishes: brushed stainless, brass, bronze, gun metal and matte black.
