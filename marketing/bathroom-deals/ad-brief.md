## Product
- **What we sell:** tapware, vanities, basins, baths and bath fillers, showers, kitchen sinks and mixers, drainage and accessories. Online, with Australian stock in our own warehouse.
- **Jobs it does:** completes a bathroom, ensuite, laundry or kitchen reno with matching, decent-quality fittings, without paying showroom prices.
- **Differentiators (proof points):**
  - We buy direct from manufacturers and wholesalers, so there's no distributor margin.
  - A shorter range, chosen on cartridge quality and finish durability.
  - Real stock in Australia: if it's listed, it's here.
  - One point of contact, a real person on 07 4271 4620.
  - Matched finishes across a whole range (e.g. Lucito in chrome, brushed stainless, black, gun metal, brushed brass and brushed bronze).
  - Warranties stated honestly, finish by finish (Lucito: 7-year finish, black 10-year body + 5-year finish).
- **What the buyer values most:** value for money with quality they can trust ("real products, real value, real savings"), then matching finishes and someone to talk to.

## Audience
1. **DIY renovators and homeowners** planning a bathroom, ensuite, laundry or kitchen reno (Brisbane/SEQ first, then Australia-wide).
   - **Drives:** a nicer home without blowing the budget; getting it right the first time.
   - **Fears:** buying the wrong size or finish; finishes that fade or mark; cheap taps that fail.
   - **Frustrations:** showroom prices; endless choice; being passed around; not knowing what fits.
   - **Trigger:** the reno is starting, or an old fitting has failed.
   - **Objections:** "Is it good quality at this price?", "Will it match?", "Can I get it here?" (vanities, tall boys and Forio sinks are collection only).
2. **Pre-sale renovators** (often Brisbane): value-adding updates for the least money, fast.
3. **Trades and small builders** (plumbers, renovators, investors): reliable stock, good prices, one person to deal with. Package quotes suit them.

**Transformation:** from a dated or broken bathroom and a confusing buying process, to a finished, matching room bought from one place at a fair price.
