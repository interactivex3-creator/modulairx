## Brand voice
Plain, direct, practical. We talk like someone who knows the range, not like an advertisement.
- **Do:** give real numbers (prices, sizes, clearances, rough-in heights); explain the trade-offs honestly; use Australian English (colour, metre, tapware, ensuite, reno); keep sentences short; name actual products from our range.
- **Don't:** use hype ("game-changer", "revolutionary", "luxury at unbeatable prices"), lots of exclamation marks, fake urgency, emoji-heavy copy (one emoji at most, social only), or US spellings.
- **Our line:** "Real products, real value, real savings." We don't dress it up.

## Compliance rules (Australian Consumer Law, not negotiable)
- **Was/now and "save $X" pricing:** only against a price the product actually sold at for a reasonable period. Never make up a reference price. If you're not sure, leave the comparison out.
- **No misleading claims:** no "cheapest in Australia", "best price guaranteed" or "lowest price" unless there's a written, checkable policy behind it.
- **Warranties:** state exactly what's covered (cartridge vs finish, how long, conditions such as licensed installation). Don't imply a warranty replaces the consumer guarantees.
- **Stock and delivery:** no "in stock" or "next-day" claims unless the product's data supports them.
- **Testimonials and reviews:** only real, verifiable ones. Never write fake reviews.
- **Plumbing:** say that installation must be done by a licensed plumber where that applies (QLD and other states require it for most fixtures).
- **WaterMark / WELS:** only quote a certification or star rating if the product data has it.
- **Email/SMS:** Spam Act 2003, so consent is required, the sender must be identified, and there must be a working unsubscribe link.
