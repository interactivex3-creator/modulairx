Small bathroom? Lift the vanity off the floor.

A wall-hung vanity leaves the floor showing underneath, so the room reads bigger and the mop goes straight under. The trade-offs: the wall needs to carry it (solid wall or noggins), and the waste and water have to come out of the wall at the right height, so plan it before the wall is lined.

Our wall-hung pick: the Nimis 900 Wall Hung Vanity, $965. 900mm wide, timber-veneer cabinet, matte white solid surface top, two soft-close drawers. It suits an above-counter or semi-inset basin (basin sold separately).

If the plumbing is already coming up through the floor, a freestanding unit is usually the easier swap. The Shaker 750mm Double Drawer Vanity is $499 with a vitreous china top and bowl and two soft-close drawers.
