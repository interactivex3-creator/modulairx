The drain that disappears into your tiles.

A tile-insert floor waste has a tray instead of a grate. Your tiler cuts a piece of the same floor tile, sets it in the tray, and the drain reads as a thin gap rather than a metal square.

Three from the Neston range, all 316 stainless steel:
- Square Tile Insert Floor Waste, DN80 outlet: $49
- Square Max Tile Insert Floor Waste, 120 x 120 x 33mm deep, made for a higher flow rate, DN100 outlet: $109
- Linear Tile Insert Drain Kit, 800mm, DN40 outlet: $229

These are the outlet sizes we have in stock now. Other sizes are currently sold out, so check before you plan around one.

Three things to sort before you order:
1. Outlet size. Ask your plumber what the waste pipe is (DN40, DN80 or DN100) and buy to match.
2. Falls. The floor has to fall to the drain. A linear drain lets the floor fall one way, which suits large-format tiles.
3. Tile thickness. The Square and Linear trays take tile up to 10mm thick; the Square Max takes up to 20mm. Check your tile before the tiler starts.

Drainage has to be installed by a licensed plumber in Queensland (and most other states). Bring them in early.
