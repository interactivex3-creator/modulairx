Measure the cupboard, not the benchtop.

One mistake our sizing guide warns about: people measure across the benchtop, but the sink and its fixings have to sit inside the cabinet underneath. Open the cupboard and measure between the side panels. Then check the cut-out size from the sink's template (it's always smaller than the rim).

Two more numbers worth knowing:
- Compact single bowls around 450mm suit smaller kitchens, studios and laundries.
- 200mm is the standard bowl depth. Deeper bowls hold more but make you lean further.
