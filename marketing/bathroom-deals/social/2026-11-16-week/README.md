## Step 1: Get the pictures onto your computer (once)
1. Open this Google Drive folder (**Week of 16 Nov 2026**).
2. Select all the **post-…jpg** files: click the first one, hold **Shift**, click the last one.
3. Right-click → **Download**. Drive gives you a zip file; open it so the pictures are in a normal folder (e.g. your Downloads folder).

## Step 2: Open the bulk scheduler
1. Go to **business.facebook.com** and pick the **Bathroom Deals** account.
2. Left menu → **Planner** (calendar icon).
3. Top right → **Schedule** dropdown (or the ⋯ menu) → **Bulk schedule posts**. You'll see the table with *Post to · Media · Text · Scheduling options*.

## Step 3: Fill in one row per post (4 rows)
Click **+ Add post** until there are 4 rows. Then for each row:
1. **Post to:** leave as **Bathroom Deals** (Facebook + Instagram).
2. **Media:** click **Add photo/video** → **Upload** → choose that post's pictures (see the table above), in order.
3. **Text:** copy that post's caption from the **Captions** section below (everything under its heading) and paste it in.
4. **Scheduling options:** click **Publish now** → change it to **Schedule** → set the date and time from the table.

When all 4 rows are done, click **Schedule** (bottom right). They'll appear in your Planner on the right days. You can still edit or delete them there before they go out.

**Tip:** if Instagram complains about the pictures in a row, untick Instagram for that row and post it to Facebook only.
