Marketing can't fix volume in one week, so the plan is: (1) put the content we have where Google already half-notices it, (2) steer the people who do arrive to a product they can buy or to a quote, (3) don't promote anything we can't ship.

## This week's 3 priorities
1. **Turn near-miss search pages into clicks.** Refresh the tapware-colours post and rewrite the product pages that real Australians are viewing. Target: pages already at positions 9–15 (tapware colours, shower-on-rail vs twin, acrylic bath guide) and the Forio, Neston stainless and Lucito pages. KPI next Monday: Australian organic sessions above 24, and the first non-brand query getting a click.
2. **Drive package-quote enquiries from social and email.** Enquiries outnumbered orders about 5 to 1 in the baseline, and the quote path suits a small catalogue with a lot of 1–2 unit lines. KPI: quote-form entries (owner to read manually, because WPForms counts aren't available to us).
3. **Only promote stock we can ship, and keep owner decisions visible.** Owner items that block growth: Neston out-of-stock decision (and un-featuring the 200×200), bot protections, Merchant feed sync (225 vs ~188). Marketing copy for next week uses in-stock products only and no stock-urgency claims.
