## Post body

Chrome, brushed stainless, black, gun metal, brushed brass or brushed bronze. Tapware colour is one of the few decisions in a bathroom you see every time you walk in, and one of the hardest to undo once the tiles are up.

This guide compares the six finishes in our Lucito range: how each looks, what it suits, how much cleaning it needs, what it costs and how long the finish warranty runs. Prices are current shop prices for the same basin mixer in each finish.

### The short answer

- **Safest, easiest to live with:** chrome or brushed stainless.
- **Strongest contrast:** black, but it has the shortest finish warranty.
- **Warm and soft:** brushed brass or brushed bronze.
- **Modern without the contrast of black:** gun metal.

The difference in price between the finishes is small. In the Lucito range the warm and dark-grey finishes cost about $30 more per basin mixer, so choose on colour, not on price.

## Chrome: the safe choice

Chrome is still the default in Australian bathrooms because it's easy to match. Replacing one tap in a room full of chrome? Stay with chrome.

It reflects light, which helps in a small ensuite or a bathroom without a window. The trade-off is that the shine shows every water spot. It wipes clean quickly, but it needs wiping more often than a brushed finish.

Chrome and black are compared head to head in [Matte Black vs Chrome Tapware](https://bathroomdeals.au/matte-black-vs-chrome-tapware-which-is-best-for-your-bathroom/), so we won't repeat it here.

## Brushed stainless: chrome without the upkeep

Brushed stainless is the most forgiving finish in the range. The satin grain hides fingerprints, light scratches and water marks far better than chrome, so it suits busy family bathrooms and kitchens.

It's a cooler, greyer tone than chrome. Put it next to chrome and the difference is obvious, so don't mix the two in the same room. Lucito brushed stainless costs the same as chrome ($169 for the Curved Spout Basin Mixer).

## Black: bold, but check the warranty

Black gives the strongest contrast against white tiles, light stone and pale timber. It also asks the most of you:

- Water marks and soap residue show up on a matte black surface, especially in hard-water areas.
- It needs gentle cleaning. Mild soapy water and a soft cloth, no abrasive pads or harsh cleaners.
- Blacks vary between brands. A black mixer from one supplier and a black rail from another often won't match in tone or sheen.

The finish warranty is shorter, too. Lucito black carries a **5-year finish warranty** and a 10-year body warranty. Every other Lucito finish carries 7 years on the finish. The difference comes from the coating process. The Lucito black basin mixer page lists an electroplated black finish.

Coatings matter more than colour names. "Matte black" can mean electroplating, PVD or powder coat depending on the brand, so read the product page and the finish warranty before you buy, not just the photo.

## Gun metal: dark without the contrast

Gun metal sits between brushed stainless and black. It reads as modern but softer than black, and it works well with grey, concrete-look and soft-neutral tiles.

The Lucito Curved Spout Basin Mixer gun metal page lists a PVD anti-fingermark finish with a 7-year finish warranty. A Curved Spout Basin Mixer is $199.

Gun metal tones vary a lot between brands, from warm brown-grey to cool blue-grey. Buy it from one range.

## Brushed brass and brushed bronze: the warm finishes

Brushed brass is the warm finish most people picture: a soft gold that sits well against timber, terrazzo, warm stone and white walls. Brushed bronze is darker and browner, and it suits earthier schemes and darker tiles where brass might look too bright.

Both are listed as PVD finishes on the Lucito basin mixer pages, with a 7-year finish warranty. Both are $199 for the Curved Spout Basin Mixer and $229 for the [Lucito Shower On Rail in brushed brass](https://bathroomdeals.au/product/lucito-shower-on-rail-brushed-brass/) or brushed bronze.

Mismatches show most in warm finishes: one brand's brushed brass can be noticeably yellower or pinker than another's. Buy tapware, shower and accessories from the same range, and don't choose on screen photos alone.

## Matching colour to the room

**Small or dark bathrooms.** Chrome and brushed stainless keep the room light. Black and bronze can feel heavy on a small wall.

**Large bathrooms and ensuites.** Black, gun metal, brass and bronze all have room to work as a feature.

**Light tiles and stone.** Black gives contrast, brass adds warmth, gun metal sits in between.

**Dark tiles.** Brushed stainless, chrome or brushed brass stop the room going flat. Black on dark tiles tends to disappear.

**Renovating around existing fittings.** Match what's staying. If the bath spout or shower stays in chrome, a chrome basin mixer will look more deliberate than one new brass tap.

## Finish warranties: what to check

A finish warranty only means something if you know what it covers and what voids it. With Lucito:

- **Black:** 5-year finish warranty, 10-year body warranty.
- **Chrome, brushed stainless, gun metal, brushed brass, brushed bronze:** 7-year finish warranty.
- Cartridge, body and labour terms are on each product page and vary by product type (for example, the Curved Spout Basin Mixer has a 20-year cartridge warranty and 5-year labour; the Shower On Rail has 10 years on parts or product and 2 years labour).
- The warranty requires installation by a licensed plumber. In Queensland and most other states, connecting tapware is licensed plumbing work anyway. Keep your plumber's compliance certificate.
- A manufacturer's warranty sits alongside your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. It doesn't replace them.

Abrasive pads and harsh cleaners damage most finishes. [The Hard Truth About Tapware Warranties in Australia](https://bathroomdeals.au/the-hard-truth-about-tapware-warranties-in-australia/) explains what finish and cartridge warranties usually do and don't cover.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the most popular tapware colour in Australia?**
Chrome is still the most common, mainly because it's easy to match with existing fittings. Black, brushed brass and gun metal are popular in new builds and full renovations.

**Which tapware finish is easiest to keep clean?**
Brushed stainless. The brushed grain hides fingerprints and water marks better than chrome, black or polished finishes.

**Does black tapware have a shorter warranty?**
In our Lucito range, yes. Black carries a 5-year finish warranty (with a 10-year body warranty), while the other five finishes carry 7 years on the finish. Always check the finish warranty on the specific product page.

**Is brushed brass or gun metal better?**
Neither is better; they do different jobs. Brushed brass adds warmth and suits timber and warm stone. Gun metal is cooler and suits grey and concrete-look schemes. In Lucito they cost the same and carry the same 7-year finish warranty.

**Is brushed nickel the same as brushed stainless?**
They look similar, but brushed nickel is usually a little warmer in tone. If you're matching to existing brushed nickel fittings, compare the two side by side before you buy, because the difference shows when they sit close together. We don't stock brushed nickel in the Lucito range.

**Should all the fittings in a bathroom be the same colour?**
For most bathrooms, yes. One finish across tapware, shower and accessories looks deliberate. Mixing can work, but it needs a plan, for example brass tapware with black accessories, used consistently.

**Does the finish change how the tap performs?**
No. For each Lucito product we checked, the body, cartridge and water rating are the same across finishes. For example, every Lucito Curved Spout Basin Mixer page lists the same 35mm cartridge and the same WELS 6 star, 4L/min rating. Only the finish, and its warranty, changes.
